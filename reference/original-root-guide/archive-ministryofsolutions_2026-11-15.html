Rooting Tutorial

  •  Backup all important data from the phone
  • Enable the oem unlock in developer options
  • Unlock the bootloader of your phone (Buy bootloader code)
  • Download root files from THIS LINK (password=ministryofsolutions.com)
  • Extract downloaded files
  • Copy the Magisk zip file to your sd card
  • Connect the phone in fastboot mode and open the MAF32.exe file
  • Type ” fastboot flash recovery_ramdisk TWRPATU.img ” and press enter
  • After successful flashing disconnect the phone from the computer
  • Hold the volume up and power
  • keep holding even after the reboot, when you see the Huawei logo leave the power button, but keep holding the volume up button, The phone will enter TWRP mode
  • Swipe to allow modifications
  • Then tap on Install and then tap on Select Storage and choose SD card
  • Find magisk zip and tap it, then swipe to flash
  • After that press reboot system, if asks to install anything press do not install.
  • Profit, Your phone has been rooted
  • Don’t update/reset the phone after the root
